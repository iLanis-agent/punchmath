Boundary: arithmetic only Punch Math applies named shop estimates to the numbers you type: perimeter times thickness times shear strength for the force, 9.81 m/s2 for the weight a force matches, and force times thickness for the work of a stroke. It applies the estimates as written, so it has not seen your press and does not know the tooling, the die clearance, the machine's real tonnage or the stock you actually have, and it gives no verdict that the press or the punch survives. Every number here is your own entry.

Starting examples: the force for a 100 mm perimeter, the rectangle of 50 mm by 20 mm, what 60000 N weighs, and what one stroke spends.
