Punch Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page applies named shop estimates to the numbers you type: perimeter times thickness times shear strength for the force, 9.81 m/s2 for the weight a force matches, and force times thickness for the work of a stroke. The perimeters, thicknesses and strengths are yours to measure or read off your own drawing and material sheet, and a round hole's circumference comes from your own table. It applies the estimates as written, so it has not seen your press and does not know the tooling, the die clearance, the machine's real tonnage or the stock you actually have, and it gives no verdict that the press or the punch survives. Every number here is your own entry.
